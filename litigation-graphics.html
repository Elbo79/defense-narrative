Common questions

Litigation graphics FAQs.

What are trial demonstratives?

They are visual aids designed to explain testimony, chronology, technical concepts, medical evidence, comparisons, or damages. The strongest demonstratives make a specific point in the case easier to understand.

Can demonstratives be used before trial?

Yes. They can be useful in mediation, settlement discussions, expert preparation, internal case analysis, and trial preparation—not only in the courtroom.

Do you create the visual and also present it in court?

Defense Narrative can connect demonstrative development with trial presentation support so the graphics are designed with the courtroom workflow and trial strategy in mind.
