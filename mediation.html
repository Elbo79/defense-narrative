Common questions

Defense mediation presentation FAQs.

What is a defense mediation presentation?

It is a visual, strategically organized presentation of the defense case designed to help mediators, carriers, counsel, and other decision-makers quickly understand the strongest liability, causation, credibility, and damages points.

When should we start?

Before the final mediation sprint. Starting early gives the team time to identify missing exhibits, chronology problems, contradictory testimony, and demonstratives worth developing.
