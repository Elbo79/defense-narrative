Common questions

Trial technician FAQs.

Does Defense Narrative provide trial technicians nationwide?

Yes. We provide trial technology support nationwide, with experienced trial technicians available locally in major markets throughout the United States. When local coverage is not available, Defense Narrative shares the travel cost.

What does a trial technician do in court?

A trial technician operates the digital presentation system and manages exhibits, deposition video, callouts, annotations, impeachment material, and demonstratives so the legal team can concentrate on advocacy.

When should defense counsel bring in a trial technician?

Before the final trial-preparation sprint whenever possible. Early involvement creates time to organize evidence, understand witness plans, build impeachment workflows, and test courtroom technology.

Is a trial technician the same as a courtroom AV vendor?

No. AV equipment is part of the infrastructure. A trial technician works directly with the litigation team to organize and present case evidence in real time.
